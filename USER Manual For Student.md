## 1. Account Creation & Login

### Step 1: Open the Application
Navigate to the platform website URL provided by your supervisor or lab lead.

### Step 2: Register as a Student
1. Click **"Register"** or **"Create Account"** on the top right.
2. Enter your details:
   - **Full Name**: Your official academic name.
   - **Email Address**: Your institutional or preferred academic email.
   - **Password**: Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters).
   - **Role**: Select **Student / Researcher**.
   - **Institution & Department**: Enter your university/department name.
3. Click **"Create Account"**.

### Step 3: Log In
1. Go to the **Login** page (`/login`).
2. Enter your registered email and password (or use GitHub / Google if configured).
3. Click **"Sign In"** to access your dashboard.

## 2. Joining Your Research Lab

To collaborate with **Basar Sir** and fellow lab members:

1. Click on **"Research Labs"** (`/labs`) in the sidebar navigation.
2. Click **"Join Lab"** button.
3. Enter the unique **Lab Join Code** provided by Basar Sir / Lab Lead.
4. (Optional) Enter a brief message stating your research topic or student ID.
5. Click **"Submit Request"**. Once approved by your supervisor, you will have access to all lab notices, sub-groups, and shared syllabi.

## 3. Exploring Your Dashboard

Your **Dashboard** (`/`) is your daily command center:
- **Reading Pipeline Counters**: Displays how many papers are *To Read*, *Reading*, and *Completed*.
- **Pending Assignments**: Highlights papers assigned by your supervisor with upcoming deadlines.
- **Active Milestones**: Shows your thesis progress bar and upcoming deliverable dates.
- **Lab Announcements**: Pins urgent conference deadlines and lab notices.
- **Quick Actions (⌘K / Ctrl+K)**: Press `Ctrl + K` anywhere to open Spotlight Search to instantly find any paper, author, or track.

## 4. Managing & Reading Research Papers

### Adding a Paper to Your Library
1. Go to **"Paper Library"** (`/papers`) and click **"+ Add Paper"** (`/papers/new`).
2. Choose your method:
   - **1-Click ArXiv Import**: Paste an ArXiv URL or ID (e.g. `2305.18290`) to auto-fill title, authors, abstract, publication year, and PDF link.
   - **DOI Lookup**: Enter a DOI to fetch metadata directly from CrossRef / Semantic Scholar.
   - **Manual Entry**: Type in the details and upload a PDF.
3. Set **Reading Status** (`To Read`, `Reading`, `Completed`) and **Priority** (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Critical`).
4. Click **"Save Paper"**.

### In-App PDF Reader & 3-Minute Digest
1. Click on any paper to open the **Paper Workspace** (`/papers/[id]`).
2. Click **"Open PDF Reader"** to read directly inside your browser without downloading.
3. Fill out the **3-Minute Digest**:
   - *Problem Solved*: What gap does this paper address?
   - *Key Contribution*: What is the novel methodology or dataset?
   - *Limitations & Failure Modes*: Where does the approach fall short?
4. Export formatted citations in **BibTeX**, **APA**, or **IEEE** format for your LaTeX papers.

## 5. Supervisor Assignments & Literature Reviews

When **Basar Sir** assigns a paper for you to review:

1. Go to **"Assignments"** (`/assignments`) from the sidebar.
2. You will see a list of assigned papers, supervisor notes, and due dates.
3. Click on the assignment to open the **Review Workspace**.
4. Read the paper and write your **Structured Literature Review**:
   - Summarize the methodology and empirical benchmark results.
   - Write down your replication notes or questions.
5. Change the status from **Pending** ➔ **In Progress** ➔ **Completed**.
6. Your supervisor will review your submission and provide feedback and rubric scores.

## 6. Thesis & Project Milestones

Track your thesis progress and semester deliverables:

1. Navigate to **"Thesis Milestones"** (`/milestones`).
2. View milestones assigned by your supervisor (e.g., *Literature Survey Chapter*, *Baseline Replication*, *Draft Defense Slides*).
3. When you finish a milestone:
   - Click **"Submit Deliverable"**.
   - Attach your Google Drive / GitHub repository link / PDF report URL.
   - Add notes explaining what was accomplished.
4. The status will update to **Submitted** and notify your supervisor for approval.

## 7. 1-on-1 Mentorship Meetings

Prepare for regular weekly check-ins with your supervisor:

1. Go to **"1-on-1 Meetings"** (`/meetings`).
2. Check your upcoming scheduled meeting date, time, and Zoom/Google Meet link.
3. **Before the Meeting**:
   - Add your **Student Agenda Notes** (e.g., experiments run this week, blockers, papers read).
4. **During & After the Meeting**:
   - Review the **Action Items** recorded by your supervisor.
   - Check off completed action items before the next meeting.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I use ResearchTrack offline?**  
A: Yes! ResearchTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA). If your internet connection drops, you can continue browsing cached papers and drafts. Your changes will sync when you reconnect.

**Q: How do I export my papers for Overleaf / LaTeX?**  
A: Go to your Paper Library or Collection, click **"Export Matrix"**, and copy the generated LaTeX table or download the `.bib` file.

**Q: Who can see my papers and notes?**  
A: Your personal library and private notes are securely stored in your account. Only papers explicitly assigned to or shared with your supervisor/lab are visible to them.
